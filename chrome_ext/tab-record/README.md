# Tab Recorder

A Chrome extension that records audio and/or video from the active browser tab.

## Install
1. Open `chrome://extensions/` in Chrome.
2. Enable "Developer mode".
3. Click "Load unpacked".
4. Select the `chrome_ext/tab-record` folder.

## Usage
1. Open the tab you want to record.
2. Click the Tab Recorder icon.
3. A separate recorder window will open.
4. Choose the tab to record from the selector.
5. Choose whether to record audio, video, or both.
6. Click "Start Recording".
7. Keep the recorder window open while recording.
8. Use the live monitor area to hear or preview the captured stream.
9. Click "Stop Recording" and then "Download".

## Notes
- Recording stops if the popup closes.
- Audio is recorded while the tab continues to play back sound normally.
- Some pages do not allow tab capture due to browser policies or protected content.
